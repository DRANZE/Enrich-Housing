About Enrich

Crafting homes with integrity, for a decade.

Welcome to Enrich Housing, a Mumbai-based real estate developer with a decade-long legacy of quality construction, timely delivery, and unwavering customer trust. We have been redefining residential and mixed-use living by creating spaces that go beyond the ordinary. Our homes are thoughtfully designed to meet the evolving aspirations of modern families — blending contemporary architecture with enduring craftsmanship.

At Enrich Housing, we believe a home is more than four walls. It is where milestones are celebrated, memories are made, and futures are built.

Every project we undertake is a reflection of our founding values — integrity, transparency, and excellence — ensuring that each homeowner receives not just a property, but a promise fulfilled.
